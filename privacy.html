Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how handslab (핸즈랩, "we", "us"), the operator of the Free GTO mobile app (the "App"), collects, uses and protects your information. We collect as little as we need to run the App.

In short: You can use every feature of the App without an account. Your practice records stay on your phone. If you sign in to join the leaderboard, we store your email, nickname, country and leaderboard scores. The App shows Google AdMob ads, and payments are handled by the App Store or Google Play.

1. Information we collect

a) Stored only on your device

Quiz answers and statistics, hand-trainer progress, best records, your nickname and country (before you sign in), language and theme settings. This information is not sent to us unless you sign in (only your nickname and country are then sent).

b) If you sign in (optional, for the leaderboard)

  • Account: the email address and account identifier provided by Google or Apple. Google or Apple may also provide your name and profile picture URL; we do not display or use them. With Sign in with Apple you may choose to hide your email.
  • Profile: the nickname and (optional) country you choose. These are shown publicly on the leaderboard.
  • Leaderboard records: your score, board type and the time it was recorded. To prevent cheating, the questions, answers and response times of a run are sent to our server and re-graded; only the resulting score is stored.
  • Technical data: our server provider records technical logs (such as IP address and request time) for security and operation, kept for a short period.

c) Advertising

The App shows banner ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information and ad interactions to show, measure and (where you allow it) personalize ads. Users in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent through Google's consent tool before personalized ads are shown. Learn more at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

d) Purchases

Support payments and memberships are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never receive your card or bank details. We use RevenueCat to manage purchases; it receives your purchase history, an app user identifier (your account identifier if you are signed in, otherwise a random identifier) and basic device information such as country and app version.

2. How we use information

  • To run the leaderboard and show your nickname, country and scores
  • To prevent cheating, abuse and inappropriate nicknames
  • To provide purchases and membership benefits (removing ads, supporter badge)
  • To show ads that keep the App free
  • To respond to your requests and keep the service secure

We do not sell your personal information. Legal bases (EEA/UK): performance of our service at your request (account, leaderboard, purchases), our legitimate interests (security, preventing cheating), and your consent (personalized ads).

4. How long we keep it

  • Account, profile and leaderboard records: until you delete your account. Deleting your account removes them from our database immediately (they may remain in backups for up to 30 days).
  • Purchase records: kept by the stores and RevenueCat as long as required for accounting and legal obligations.
  • Data on your device: until you reset it in the App or uninstall the App.

5. Your rights and choices

  • Delete your account anytime in the App: Me → Delete account.
  • Change your nickname or country in Me → Edit profile.
  • Access, correct, delete or restrict processing of your data, object to processing, or request a copy: email us at bionje2025@gmail.com.
  • Ads: you can change ad consent (EEA/UK) and reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings.
  • You may complain to your local data protection authority.

6. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (under 16 in the EEA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Security

We use encrypted connections, access controls on our database (each user can only change their own profile, and scores can only be saved after server-side checking), and keep the information we collect to a minimum. No method is 100% secure, but we work to protect your data.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. We will post the new version here with a new effective date and, for important changes, notify you in the App.
